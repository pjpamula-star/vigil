Vigil — first look

Four places you'll see it: the tray icon, the tray panel, the "While you were away" screen, and your phone. Click an option in sections 2 and 3 to pick one.

1 · The tray icon tells you the state at a glance

An eye in the system tray (next to the clock). If it's dim, your PC behaves normally. If it's lit, Vigil is holding it awake, and you always know why.
